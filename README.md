# life-in-pixel
Life in Pixels is an interactive 3D web application that turns your personal memories, daily habits, and life milestones into an evolving pixel-art island or floating star constellation. It brings your life story to life through dynamic color palettes, interactive visual growth, and a time-lapse replay mode—all kept 100% private in your browser.
