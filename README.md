# Life in Pixels

### Your life, visualized one moment at a time.

Life in Pixels is an interactive 3D experience that transforms everyday moments, personal memories, and meaningful milestones into a living visual representation of your journey.

Built around the idea that every moment contributes to a bigger picture, the project reimagines how people reflect on their lives. Instead of conventional timelines and productivity dashboards, it creates an evolving digital world that grows alongside your experiences.

Explore your journey through two distinct visual environments:

- **Pixel Island:** A dynamic pixel-art world that evolves as your experiences accumulate.
- **Star Constellation:** An immersive constellation where individual moments become part of a larger visual story.

### What Makes It Different

- Interactive 3D environments that bring personal experiences to life.
- Evolving visual landscapes that reflect the passage of time.
- Multiple visualization modes for exploring your personal journey.
- Dynamic colours and visual elements that create an expressive experience.
- Time-lapse replay to revisit the evolution of your world.
- A browser-based experience designed with personal data privacy in mind.

### The Idea Behind It

Life is not defined by a single achievement or milestone. It is shaped by countless little moments, experiences, and memories that gradually become something meaningful.

Life in Pixels turns that idea into an interactive experience, giving those moments a visual identity and a world of their own.

**Every moment leaves a mark. Every pixel tells a story.**
